# *A Molecular Survey of Antifungal Susceptibility Patterns and a Selected Virulence Factor of Candida albicans from Mzingwane and Mtshabezi Dams*
[🔬 View Project: Antifungal Resistance in Candida albicans](https://github.com/MthandazoMpofu/mthandazompofu/blob/main/ANTIFUNGAL%20RESISTANCE%20IN%20CANDIDA%20ALBICANS.pdf)

This four-month investigation examined antifungal susceptibility patterns and molecular markers associated with antifungal resistance and virulence in environmental *Candida albicans* isolates.

## Areas of focus:

* Microbiology

## Laboratory
Kirby-Bauer susceptibility testing, DNA extraction, PCR, agarose gel electrophoresis and PCR optimisation.

## Analysis
Data recording, interpretation of susceptibility patterns, molecular results and comparison with published evidence.

*100%* reported fluconazole resistance among tested isolates
*85%* reported itraconazole resistance among tested isolates
*50%* reported posaconazole resistance among tested isolates

## Molecular Investigation
Nine isolates resistant to all three antifungals were selected for molecular analysis. ERG11, MDR1 and ALS1 were investigated as resistance and virulence-associated genes.

## Research Interpretation
The study considered environmental contamination, wastewater, agricultural azole exposure and other possible contributors to observed resistance patterns.
